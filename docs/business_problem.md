# Business Problem

## Context

A large retail company with a Walmart-like operating model sells thousands of products across multiple stores, categories, departments, and states. Demand changes every day due to seasonality, promotions, price changes, holidays, local events, consumer behavior, and product availability.

The company currently relies on historical averages and manual planning rules to estimate future demand. This creates operational risk because planners do not have a consistent and data-driven way to anticipate which products will need more inventory, which stores may face stockouts, and which categories may carry excess inventory.

## Business Challenge

The organization needs a forecasting system that can estimate future unit demand at different business levels:

- Product and store
- Product category
- Department
- State
- Total retail network

The forecast should support inventory planning, replenishment decisions, purchasing cycles, and commercial planning. The solution must be explainable enough for business stakeholders and accurate enough to improve operational decision-making.

## Analytical Objective

Use historical Walmart sales data from the M5 Forecasting Accuracy competition to build a professional demand forecasting workflow. The project will start with data understanding and analytical foundations, then progress toward baseline forecasting, machine learning models, model evaluation, visual analytics, and business recommendations.

## Business Value

A successful forecasting workflow can help the company:

- Reduce stockout risk for high-demand products.
- Lower excess inventory and holding costs for slow-moving products.
- Improve purchasing and replenishment timing.
- Identify seasonal and event-driven demand patterns.
- Prioritize operational attention by product, category, store, and state.
- Translate predictive analytics into practical retail planning actions.
